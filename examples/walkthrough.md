## 2. Authored example answer

The two known facts justify proceeding to check this example's remaining conditions. They should not be discarded just because other data are missing. Modern claims such as managerial ability cannot be invented from star names.

Minimum additions: the full relevant passage and readable page from the same edition; the natal Life Palace, three-party and opposite-palace star placements, including auxiliary and adverse stars; and the birth-year stem and source of natal transformations. Real names, addresses, and period charts are unnecessary for this narrow question.

**Plain-language summary:** The basic combination is present. The supporting conditions remain unchecked, so a complete pattern or future wealth cannot yet be asserted. Supply the passage and related placements to establish how far the interpretation can go.
